title: Add HourGlass to your Safe
description: Install the HourGlass Safe App and open it from your Safe.
---

HourGlass runs as a [Safe App](https://app.safe.global) inside your Safe's
interface, so charges are authorized by your existing Safe signers.

## Install

1. Open your Safe at [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global).
2. Go to **Apps** and add a custom app.
3. Use the HourGlass app URL:

```
https://hourglass.box
```

Safe reads the app manifest from that URL and loads HourGlass in a sandboxed
iframe. The same URL serves the public site in a normal browser tab and the Safe
App when loaded inside Safe.
